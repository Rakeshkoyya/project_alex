# Photosynthesis

## Foundations: cells and organelles
All living things are made of cells. Plant cells have a cell wall, a cell membrane, cytoplasm, a nucleus and special organelles called chloroplasts. Chloroplasts contain the green pigment chlorophyll, which absorbs light. Animal cells do not have chloroplasts, which is why animals cannot make their own food.

## Foundations: energy and its forms
Energy is the ability to do work. It comes in forms such as light energy, heat energy and chemical energy. Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only changed from one form to another (conservation of energy). Food stores chemical energy in the bonds between atoms.

## Foundations: matter, atoms and chemical reactions
Matter is made of atoms. Atoms join to form molecules, for example water (H2O), carbon dioxide (CO2) and oxygen (O2). In a chemical reaction the reactants are rearranged into new products; atoms are not lost, so equations must balance.

## What photosynthesis is
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae and some bacteria use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen as a by-product. It converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.

Word equation: carbon dioxide + water → (light, chlorophyll) → glucose + oxygen

Balanced equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

## Where it happens
Photosynthesis happens mainly in the leaves, inside chloroplasts in the palisade mesophyll cells. Carbon dioxide enters through tiny pores called stomata; water travels up from the roots through xylem vessels. Guard cells open and close the stomata.

## The two stages
1. Light-dependent reactions (in the thylakoid membranes): chlorophyll absorbs light, water is split (photolysis), oxygen is released, and the energy carriers ATP and NADPH are made.
2. Light-independent reactions / Calvin cycle (in the stroma): ATP and NADPH are used to fix carbon dioxide into glucose.

## Limiting factors
The rate of photosynthesis depends on light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature. Whichever factor is in shortest supply limits the rate. Too high a temperature denatures the enzymes involved.

## Why it matters
Photosynthesis is the start of almost every food chain, it produces the oxygen we breathe, and it removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. Respiration is roughly the reverse process: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy.
