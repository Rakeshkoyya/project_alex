# Fractions from the ground up

## Foundations: division and equal parts
A fraction describes equal parts of a whole. Dividing 1 pizza into 4 equal slices makes each slice one quarter. Division means sharing equally: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 means 12 shared into 3 equal groups gives 4 in each.

## Numerator and denominator
In 3/4 the denominator (4) says how many equal parts the whole is cut into; the numerator (3) says how many of those parts we have. A fraction a/b also means a ÷ b.

## Equivalent fractions
Multiplying or dividing the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number gives an equivalent fraction: 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8. Simplifying means dividing both by their greatest common factor: 6/8 = 3/4.

## Comparing fractions
With the same denominator, the larger numerator is bigger. Otherwise rewrite both with a common denominator (use the least common multiple): compare 2/3 and 3/4 as 8/12 and 9/12.

## Adding and subtracting
Only add numerators when denominators match: 1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5. Otherwise convert first: 1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

## Multiplying and dividing
Multiply straight across: 2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15. To divide, multiply by the reciprocal: 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

## Mixed numbers
2 1/3 means 2 + 1/3 = 7/3. Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions before multiplying or dividing.
